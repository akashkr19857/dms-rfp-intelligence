# Data sources

## Public RFP / tender documents (`data/rfps/`)
Public procurement documents published by US local governments on their own websites. They are public records, used here
unchanged for demonstration and evaluation only. All rights remain with the issuing agencies.

| File | Issuer | Original URL |
|---|---|---|
| `champaign_CCMS.pdf` | Champaign County, Illinois – RFP2025-007 Court Case Management System (2025) | https://champaigncountyil.gov/bid_documents/2025/RFP2025-007_Court_Case_Management_System/ |
| `detroit_RFP184578.pdf` | City of Detroit – RFP 184578 Physical Records Management Software | https://detroitmi.gov/ (Office of Contracting and Procurement) |
| `tacoma_PI24-0143F.pdf` | City of Tacoma – PI24-0143F FERC Document Management System | https://cityoftacoma.org/ (Purchasing, formal bids) |

`data/download_data.py` fetches them again if they are missing.

## Organisational knowledge base (`data/knowledge_base/`)
**Fictional.** "Nimbus JusticeTech Solutions, Inc.", its NimbusCourt product, customers, references and staff are invented
for this demo and generated by `data/make_knowledge_base.py`. Any resemblance to real companies or people is coincidental.
The content is designed to meet some Champaign County requirements and miss others, so the compliance and risk analysis
has known ground truth.
